HOUSE CUP MINI
==============


Recommended slicer setup
------------------------
Printer: Prusa MK3.9S
Nozzle: 0.4 mm
Layer height: 0.20 mm (0.15 mm is fine for cleaner plaque text)
Perimeters: 3
Top/bottom solid layers: 4
Infill: 10-15% gyroid
SUPPORTS: OFF

The 6.4 mm magnet-pocket roofs are intentionally short bridges. A MK3.9S should
bridge these without removable support material. If you enable automatic
supports everywhere, PrusaSlicer may put support inside the magnet pockets even
though it is not necessary.


Parts
-----
House_Cup_Mini_base.stl       print 1
House_Cup_Mini_cup.stl        print 1, upright as exported
House_Cup_Mini_handle.stl     print 2, flat as exported
House_Cup_Mini_plaque.stl     print 1, text facing up
House_Cup_Mini_G.stl          print 1 if desired
House_Cup_Mini_S.stl          print 1 if desired
House_Cup_Mini_R.stl          print 1 if desired
House_Cup_Mini_H.stl          print 1 if desired


Magnets
-------
Nominal magnet: 6 mm diameter x 2 mm thick neodymium disc
Modeled pocket: 6.4 mm diameter x ~2.25 mm deep
Plaque: 2 magnets
Each house badge: 2 magnets
10 magnets total if all four house badges are prepared.


Assembly
--------
1. Dry-fit cup into base.
2. Dry-fit handles into the cup sockets, then use a small amount of medium CA.
3. Dry-fit plaque locating features, then glue plaque to base with medium CA.
4. Glue the two plaque magnets first and allow them to cure.
5. Let two loose magnets snap to the plaque magnets. Mark the exposed faces.
6. Glue those magnets into each house badge without flipping polarity.


Filament suggestion
-------------------
Cup + handles: antique/silk gold or bronze PLA
Base: black PLA
Plaque: gold PLA
House shields: red / green / blue / yellow PLA
